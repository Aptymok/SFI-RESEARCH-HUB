# Permission request — A-EMP-001

Suggested recipient: Michal Kozubek, CTC Main Coordinator — kozubek@fi.muni.cz
Suggested CC: Martin Maška — xmaska@fi.muni.cz

Subject: Permission request for non-CTC scientific use of Fluo-N2DL-HeLa

Dear Prof. Kozubek and Dr. Maška,

We are conducting a non-commercial research project at System Friction Institute on operational individuation and temporal identity reconstruction in dynamic systems. We would like to use the Cell Tracking Challenge Fluo-N2DL-HeLa training dataset, including the available tracking/reference annotations and imperfect segmentation masks, as an external benchmark.

The purpose is not to clone, redistribute, or republish the CTC dataset. Raw images and annotations would remain in controlled local storage and would not be committed to our public GitHub repository. The public research record would contain source links, provenance/checksums where appropriate, analysis code, methods, and only lawful derived results. We will cite and acknowledge the Cell Tracking Challenge and the relevant CTC publications/data providers as required.

Our intended analysis asks whether temporal persistence/history improves out-of-sample reconstruction of object continuity relative to instantaneous-similarity baselines. The CTC reference annotations would be isolated from feature/model construction and used only for evaluation after predictions are generated.

Could you please confirm whether you grant permission for this public, non-commercial, non-CTC scientific use of Fluo-N2DL-HeLa under the conditions above, and advise if any additional attribution, data-provider consent, or restrictions apply?
