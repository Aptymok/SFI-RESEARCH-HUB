# PROTOCOL-A-AI-CONFIRMATORY-GATE-001

Status: PROSPECTIVE DESIGN — NOT YET CONFIRMATORY
Date: 2026-08-20

## Research question

Can operational continuity of an AI system across controlled transformations be measured more accurately and usefully by a boundary-explicit combination of relational, lineage, response, authority and temporal evidence than by established single-axis or ensemble baselines?

## Unit of analysis

A paired observation (A,B) of an AI system configuration under a declared observation boundary Omega and declared analytical purpose p.

Decision space:

H_id(A,B | Omega,p) in {SAME, DISTINCT, UNRESOLVED}

## Purposes to adjudicate separately

P1 accountability continuity
P2 functional continuity
P3 authorization continuity
P4 memory/history continuity
P5 deployment/service continuity

No universal identity label is assumed.

## Experimental structure

Phase DEV:
- visible perturbation families;
- tune engineering implementation only;
- estimate variance;
- identify broken scenarios;
- no confirmatory claims.

Phase LOCK:
- freeze scenario generator version;
- freeze feature definitions;
- freeze baselines;
- freeze metric family;
- freeze delta_min;
- freeze exclusion rules;
- freeze seeds where deterministic control is possible;
- hash all frozen artifacts.

Phase TEST:
- execute held-out perturbation combinations not used for rule construction;
- collect blinded independent adjudications;
- score all baselines and SFI-A without post-hoc threshold changes;
- preserve all negative and unresolved results.

Phase TRANSFER:
- only after TEST;
- evaluate a structurally different domain or implementation family without changing the frozen identity rule;
- biology remains an optional adversarial transfer domain, not primary evidence.

## Baselines

B0 identifier continuity
B1 model/version continuity
B2 memory/state continuity
B3 behavioral-response similarity
B4 authority continuity
B5 provenance/lineage continuity
B6 strongest pre-registered ensemble of B0-B5

SFI-A must be compared primarily against B6, not merely weak individual baselines.

## Primary endpoint

Macro-averaged purpose-specific classification performance including UNRESOLVED, with uncertainty intervals and calibration analysis.

Secondary endpoints:
- future-response prediction conditional on present state;
- disagreement structure across purposes;
- robustness to identifier permutation;
- robustness to irrelevant metadata changes;
- history ablation;
- boundary expansion sensitivity;
- transfer degradation.

## Falsification / downgrade rules

F1: If B6 matches or exceeds SFI-A within the frozen equivalence margin, novelty is not supported.
F2: If performance depends materially on identifiers or labels after permutation, relational invariance is not supported.
F3: If history ablation causes no material held-out decrement, trajectory memory is not supported as necessary in that tested regime.
F4: If boundary expansion reverses conclusions frequently without predictable uncertainty, boundary robustness is not supported.
F5: If adjudicator reliability remains inadequate after purpose and boundary specification, report underdetermination rather than force identity labels.
F6: If gains disappear on transfer, claims remain domain-specific.

## Leakage controls

Adjudicators must not see SFI-A scores or baseline outputs.
The scoring implementation must not consume adjudication labels from TEST before final scoring.
Scenario metadata revealing intended perturbation class must be hidden where it would trivialize adjudication.
No TEST scenario may be used to change feature definitions or thresholds.

## Required human gate

Before Phase LOCK, independent adjudicators and the minimum acceptable inter-rater reliability criterion must be specified. This is a human governance decision because the ground-truth construct is purpose-dependent and cannot be generated by the SFI-A scorer itself.

## Interpretation ceiling

Even a positive confirmatory result supports only the measurement claim for the tested purposes, implementations and perturbation regime. It does not establish metaphysical identity, consciousness, personhood, or System Friction Theory.
