## Evidence currently in the repository

Synthetic datasets and scripts are present only to test implementation, identifiability, scoring, and reproducibility. They are not evidence that corresponding claims hold in the world.

### Empirical
No empirical dataset is mirrored in this public repository. Candidate external sources are documented by provenance and access rules. Empirical analysis begins only after selection criteria and protocol gates are frozen.
