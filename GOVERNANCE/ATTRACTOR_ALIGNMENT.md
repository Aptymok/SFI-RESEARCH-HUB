# Research Attractor Alignment

Status: active

The declared attractor is not a claim that SFI must be correct. It is a constraint on research scope: develop falsifiable, observable, longitudinal accounts of persistence, transition, intervention cost, and reconfiguration across complex human, technical, biological, or hybrid systems.

## Alignment dimensions

Each proposed branch is scored 0-5 on:
- A1 Observability: can the relevant state/change be measured?
- A2 Falsifiability: can evidence count against the claim?
- A3 Longitudinality: does time/persistence matter to the question?
- A4 Cross-domain transfer potential: could the construct be tested beyond one case?
- A5 Non-circularity: can the work fail without redefining SFI to survive?
- A6 Reproducibility/provenance: can another investigator reconstruct the chain?
- A7 Strategic field relevance: does it contribute to complex systems, HCI/human-AI, AI governance/observability, measurement, or adjacent target fields?

Alignment score = sum(A1..A7) / 35 * 100.

## Execution rule

- >=80%: priority branch; execute if resources permit.
- 65-79%: conditional branch; execute only with strong information gain or external opportunity.
- 50-64%: observation-only; log but do not commit major resources.
- <50%: reject or archive unless the attractor itself is explicitly revised.

## Branch value model

Execution Priority (EP) = 0.35*Alignment + 0.25*ExpectedInformationGain + 0.15*FalsificationPower + 0.15*ExternalTiming + 0.10*ResourceFit.

All inputs are scored 0-100.

The purpose is to prevent novelty, publication pressure, or aesthetic appeal from pulling the program into unrelated research.

## Divergence rule

A branch that is interesting but below threshold is not deleted. It is moved to Observatory/Backlog with the reason it was not executed. This preserves emergent signals without consuming the research program.
