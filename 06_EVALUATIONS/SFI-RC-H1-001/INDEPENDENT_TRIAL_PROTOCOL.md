# Reality Chain H1 — prospective independent comparison protocol (not executed)

**Status:** PRE-REGISTRATION CANDIDATE; no participants recruited, no assignments made, no outcomes. 
**Parent:** SFI-RC-2026-METHOD-001, hypothesis H1 (typed evidence-chain reconstructibility advantage).
**Existing self-test:** [SFI-RC-H1-001](README.md). Its curated Knight claims are **excluded from the confirmatory primary holdout** to avoid leakage from exploratory work.

## Question, estimand and comparison

Given identical documentary evidence, equal reading time and identical reconstruction instructions, does a typed evidence-chain representation improve **correctly supported historical reconstruction** compared with conventional documentary source citation and chronological note-taking?

A: conventional provenance workflow, including source title, date, URL, passage citation and chronological notes. 
B: Reality Chain typed record, additionally distinguishing event time, source publication time, observation, inference, authorization, execution, RETURN, correction and uncertainty. 
Both arms receive the same texts, metadata, word limits and time budget. The extra structure is the intervention; increased amount of information is not.

**Primary metric:** fraction of predefined event-claim items correctly supported by a temporally admissible source, with source locator, at a preregistered historical cut-off. Unsupported claims and retrospective leaks are errors; justified abstentions score as correct when an item has no admissible source. Scoring key is fixed before participants are exposed to the packets.

**Secondary metrics:** temporal leaks per reconstruction, unjustified causal claims, proven false closures, omissions, justified abstentions, completion time, adjudicator inter-rater agreement and sensitivity to source volume and cutoff choice. All items and adjudication criteria must be pre-specified.

## Design / safeguards

1. Independent custodians select at least two public documentary incidents besides Knight, with contemporary and later regulatory or postmortem source vintages. Cases and full packet identities are frozen before assignment.
2. Independent assessors prepare an evidence-key and blind duplicate scoring with disagreements logged; an assessor must not score their own reconstruction.
3. Randomize eligible participants to A or B; use same training length, materials and time limit. Log exclusions, experience, revisions and unexpected access; preregister participant target from a power calculation before recruitment rather than fabricate a convenient N.
4. Disallow post-cutoff documents for historical reconstruction where those documents were unavailable; retain full unfiltered corpus separately for retrospective benchmark scoring.
5. Compare within-case and across-case results with uncertainty intervals and case-level clustering; publish negative, null and contradictory results and all deviations.
6. Do not claim a causal benefit without assignment fidelity, independence and comparable resource budgets.
7. No private source data, operational credentials, human participant identifiers or synthetic observations are part of the public record.

## Falsification and decision

H1 is weakened when the matched conventional arm performs as well as or better than the typed arm within uncertainty and reasonable resource budgets, or improvements are explained by extra text, training, source count, scoring bias or time expenditure. An explicit non-significant result is not to be relabeled as evidence of a superiority claim.

**Gate to execute:** reviewer/custodian eligibility, lawful source packet, frozen independent scoring key, preregistration timestamp and authority for participant research. No such completion or result is claimed by this protocol.
