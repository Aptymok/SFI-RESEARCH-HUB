## Current external research landscape

The surrounding field is moving toward continuous AI observability, distributed traces, evaluation, governance, coordination transparency and monitoring of non-deterministic/agentic systems. This raises strategic relevance for SFI-B but also raises the novelty bar: 'AI observability matters' is no longer novel. The research must discriminate a specific measurable contribution such as state-transition reconstructability, lineage structure, or governance/observability non-equivalence.

A second adjacent line in 2026 frames human-AI governance dynamically in terms of authority, autonomy, accountability and trust rather than static human-in-the-loop categories. This increases the relevance of longitudinal state representations but means SFI-B must engage these frameworks rather than claim an empty field.
