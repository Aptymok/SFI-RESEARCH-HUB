# Action required from the founder

This file contains only actions that cannot be manufactured by the repository.

## 1. Freeze authorship metadata
For each research object, provide:
- canonical author names;
- ORCID identifiers;
- affiliation wording that is accurate at submission time;
- corresponding-author email intended for publication.

Do not place identifying metadata in the CHI anonymous export.

## 2. Select lawful empirical evidence for SFI-A
Choose one longitudinal dataset/domain where entity boundaries or continuity events have an independently defensible reference. Record provenance, license/permission, date range, variables, and why it was selected before analysis.

Do not use a dataset merely because it produces the desired result.

## 3. Select lawful workflow episodes for SFI-B
Provide or authorize a bounded set of human-AI workflow episodes that can legally and ethically be analyzed. Public GitHub must never receive confidential INEGI, personal, credential, secret, or restricted records. If real institutional data are used, they remain outside the public repo and only approved de-identified derivatives may be released.

## 4. Decide CHI contribution object
Before submission, choose whether CHI evaluates a method, system, empirical finding, or theoretical contribution. Do not submit a generic SFT manifesto. The CHI paper must stand as HCI work without requiring acceptance of SFT.

## 5. Freeze SFT-0.1
After reviewing the current specification for claims you are genuinely willing to have falsified, create an immutable timestamp/release for the specification. Do not silently edit frozen hypotheses after empirical results are observed.

## 6. External validation
Identify at least one person not involved in constructing the hypotheses to review protocols, scoring rules, and negative controls before confirmatory execution.

## 7. What NOT to do
- Do not invent empirical data.
- Do not backfill results into the hypothesis ledger.
- Do not treat synthetic benchmarks as evidence for the world.
- Do not publish confidential source data.
- Do not create a Zenodo DOI merely because a folder exists.
- Do not claim SFT is validated from A/B by construction.
